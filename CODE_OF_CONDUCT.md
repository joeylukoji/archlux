# Code of conduct

archlux adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise ideas and code, never people.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests,
reviews or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through a
[GitHub private report](https://github.com/joeylukoji/archlux/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`. Reports are read by the
maintainers only and handled confidentially. Enforcement follows the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
