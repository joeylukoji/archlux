- **B1, valid before correction** (article_wrong; formula: valid_before over cases). denominator is 270 cases, not 540
- **B1, legalize alone valid after** (consistent; formula: valid_after over pavage=False). the Wilson interval treats the 3 amplitudes of a plan as independent

- **B3, azimuths reaching sign agreement 0.80 (analytic_untrained)** (consistent; formula: sign_agreement >= 0.80). 20 points per azimuth: the 0.80 threshold is tested with a wide interval
- **B4, coverage, exchangeable** (consistent; formula: coverage per run). a mean over 20 draws of synthetic two-room plans; not a guarantee for the selected regime (exchangeability broken by the optimizer)
- **B4, coverage, selected** (consistent; formula: coverage per run). a mean over 20 draws of synthetic two-room plans; not a guarantee for the selected regime (exchangeability broken by the optimizer)
- **B4, paired Wilcoxon passive vs selected** (consistent; formula: scipy.stats.wilcoxon over the 20 paired runs). a non-significant difference on 20 draws is not evidence of no effect
- **B6, dwellings read / kept** (ambiguous; formula: LoadStatistics summary line). load_msd(limit=400) stops at the 400th kept dwelling: 1 247 is the number read until then, not the size of the corpus, and 32.1 % is not a corpus-wide retention rate (the loader documents 22 % on the full corpus). No raw rows: the summary is all that is published.
- **B6, valid before / after legalize (idempotence)** (ambiguous; formula: summary lines). idempotence is tested on the 398 valid plans; the 2 invalid ones were repaired, which is not idempotence. Summary file only (cannot_verify against raw rows).
- **B6, number of corruptions** (consistent; formula: distinct (plan_id, fault, amplitude)). the 300 repair dwellings are the first 300 of the 400 kept for idempotence (same loader, same order), not 300 other dwellings; table 1's '400 + 4 796' should read '400 dwellings (idempotence); 4 796 corruptions of 300 of them'
- **B6, legalize / tiling / fallback valid after** (consistent; formula: fallback = tiling valid, else legalize when tiling refuses for any reason). fallback only on an unrecoverable grid (status 'trame'): 93.5 %. The Wilson interval treats the 4 796 corruptions as independent; the dwelling-cluster bootstrap is the honest one.
- **B6, McNemar tiling vs legalize** (ambiguous; formula: exact binomial on discordant pairs). McNemar assumes independent pairs; the 16 corruptions of a dwelling are not. The direction is not in doubt, the p-value's magnitude is overstated.
- **B7, dwellings; train / calibration / test** (cannot_verify; formula: summary line). matches the summary; no raw prediction rows are published (Swiss Dwellings not redistributed), so the R2 / MAE / coverage values cannot be recomputed
- **B7, within-dwelling share of variance** (cannot_verify; formula: 1 - R2(apartment identity)). summary file only; the 'variance reduced thirteen-fold' is 1/0.077 = 13
