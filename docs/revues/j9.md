# Review of milestone 9: variants by sun orientation

## Criterion as written

Milestone 9 has no `MILESTONE-9.md`; `experiences/j9_orientation.py` says what it
shows (every variant is a certified valid plan) and what it does not (the optimized
objective is not real daylight: `SubstitutAnalytique` ranks rooms at
`rho = +0.085` against simulated Swiss Dwellings rooms, and inverts on held-out sites).
The audit found degenerate rooms in the figure (sides of 0.50 m, rooms of 0.7 m²).

## Replayed?

**No.** It reads the generated plans of milestone 8 (JSONL, not reachable from the CI),
and PLAN.md ties its replay to **phase 6.1**, a per-room surrogate: replaying it with
the surrogate that produced 0.7 m² rooms would reproduce the defect, not review it.

What phase 1 and this review already change for it:

- the degenerate rooms came from an objective that pushed rooms to the minimum width
  with no minimum area. Milestone 3's replay shows the mechanism on a synthetic plan:
  1 m² rooms without minimum areas, none under 12 m² with them
  ([j3](j3.md)); batch 1.2 keeps every Frank-Wolfe iterate above its minimum areas;
- the orientation invariance is on objective values, not plans ([j3](j3.md)): variants
  of a flat optimum can differ at equal predicted daylight.

**Command for the local run** (after phase 6.1):

```
make resultats-corpus HD=/path/to/j8_plans.jsonl
```

## Decision

- **Suspended** until phase 6.1. The figure must be regenerated with a per-room
  surrogate **and** minimum areas; the criterion becomes: no room under its minimum
  area, no side under `largeur_min`, and each variant labelled "what the surrogate
  believes", never "what the light does".
