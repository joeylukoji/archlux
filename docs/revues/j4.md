# Review of milestone 4: learned surrogate and gradient checkpoint

## Criterion as written

`MILESTONE-4.md` §0, two conditions, the second one blocking:

```python
assert mae(reseau, JEU_TEST) < mae(ANALYTIQUE, JEU_TEST)
rapport = ax.light.valider_gradient(reseau, ORACLE, echantillon(80), CTX)
assert rapport.accord_de_signe > 0.80  # lang-ok: quoted; "do NOT go on to milestone 5"
```

`ORACLE` is `SplitFluxOracle`, a closed form; the network learns the residual
`SplitFluxOracle − SubstitutAnalytique`, two closed forms (AUDIT.md: "the checkpoint
was crossed on a criterion that could not fail").

## Replayed?

Yes, on revision `323d1a3` and later: `python experiences/j4_gradient.py` (rewritten in
English, 49 lines), `resultats/j4_gradient.csv`, byte-stable, seed 17. As written this
time:

- the MAE is measured on a **held-out test set** of 80 plans. The published
  `mae_reseau = 0.0175` and `mae_analytique = 6.4007` were measured on the 36
  **training** plans, and **no longer reproduce**: the old script, re-run on revision
  `4861f4c` with its own seed and data, gives 0.3272 and 41.8926. They came from an
  earlier oracle or surrogate, not from the code that ships;
- the sign agreement is measured on **80 points**, 20 at each of four azimuths. The
  published 1.000, and `test_acceptation_jalon4.py`, used 3 and 4 points, **all at
  south**;
- control: the **untrained** analytic surrogate takes the same checkpoint.

## Result

| Surrogate | MAE on test | Sign agreement at 0° | 90° | 180° | 270° |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| dense network (trained) | **1.19** (old script today: 0.33 on training; published 0.0175 does not reproduce) | 0.68 | 0.50 | 1.00 | 0.67 |
| analytic, untrained | 37.3 (old script today: 41.9) | 0.67 | 0.67 | 1.00 | 0.67 |

- **Condition 1 holds** on a test set: 1.19 < 37.3.
- **Condition 2, the checkpoint, fails** at three azimuths out of four. It passed only
  where it was measured, at south.
- **The checkpoint does not measure learning**: the untrained analytic surrogate has
  the same profile. The failures are not reversed signs but **missing sensitivities**:
  at a 6 m cut, the analytic gradient is exactly 0 on the room depth at 0° (oracle:
  +0.90) and on the width at 90° and 270° (oracle: +0.59). The network, analytic plus a
  learned residual, inherits these zeros.
- Whatever the result, the oracle is a closed form: a sign agreement with it says
  nothing about daylight.

## Decision

- **Checkpoint reopened.** The milestone 4 checkpoint is **not passed**, and nothing
  downstream (milestone 5 and later) may cite it as passed. The replay as written is in
  the suite as a strict `xfail`
  (`test_acceptation_jalon4.py::test_gradient_checkpoint_as_written_on_80_points_at_four_azimuths`):
  it turns red when the checkpoint passes, forcing this report to be updated.
- **Rewritten criterion**: sign agreement measured against a **physical simulation**
  (phase 6.2, Radiance or measured labels), on **real plans** (phase 6.1), at all
  azimuths, and compared with the untrained analytic surrogate as a baseline: a learned
  surrogate must beat it.
- `SubstitutInvalide.report` now carries the failing report, so that a failed check is
  recorded instead of parsed from a message.
- Traced in [ADR 0002](../adr/0002-milestone-criteria-rewritten.md).
