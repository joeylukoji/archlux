# Review of milestone 5: conformal bound and complete certificate

## Criterion as written

`MILESTONE-5.md` §0:

```python
bornes  = [modele.borne(p) for p in JEU_TEST]
verites = [ORACLE.evaluer(p, CTX) for p in JEU_TEST]   # SplitFluxOracle
couv = np.mean([v >= b.borne for v, b in zip(verites, bornes)])
assert 0.86 <= couv <= 0.94          # target 0.90
```

"The upper bound matters as much as the lower one": a coverage of 0.97 signals
intervals that are too wide. PLAN.md adds two checks: coverage on plans **selected by
the optimizer**, and the interval width relative to the spread of the target.

The criterion as written is **one-sided** (`v >= b.borne`), while
`test_acceptation_jalon5.py` measures a **two-sided** interval
(`borne_inf <= v <= borne_sup`). Against the lower end of a two-sided 90 % interval, the
one-sided coverage is about 0.95, outside the band: the test measured the reading that
could pass. This review keeps the two-sided reading, the one the report publishes.

## Result

| Regime | Mean coverage (20 seeds) | Min – max | Seeds within [0.86, 0.94] | Width / target std |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| exchangeable (held-out plans) | **0.903** | 0.864 – 0.943 | 18 / 20 | 4.2 – 5.0 (median 4.8) |
| selected (chosen by Frank-Wolfe) | 0.879 | **0.787** – 0.938 | 14 / 20 | 4.0 – 4.9 (median 4.6) |

- **Exchangeable: the guarantee holds.** Mean 0.903 for a nominal 0.90. Two seeds out
  of 20 fall just outside the band: with 220 calibration and 280 test points the
  coverage of one run has a standard deviation of about 0.027, so a ±0.04 band fails
  about one run in seven while the method is exact. The band is a test of one draw, not
  of the method.
- **Selected: the guarantee does not hold**, as batch 1.6 declared (winner's curse):
  0.879 on average, 0.787 on the worst seed. The regime label `"selected"` and the
  report's refusal to print a coverage for such a plan are justified by measurement.
- **The interval is uninformative**: 4 to 5 times the standard deviation of the target.
  "The true value is in this interval" says less than "the true value is somewhere in
  the usual range of plans". The cause is known (AUDIT.md §5.5): `incertitude` is
  constant in every shipped surrogate, so `σ` does not shrink where the model is good.
- The certificate section the audit found missing (`Certificat.performance`) is filled
  by `legalize(..., calibration=)` since batch 1.6.

## Decision

- **Coverage criterion kept, measured on its distribution**: mean coverage over at least
  20 calibration seeds within 0.90 ± 0.02, instead of one run within ±0.04.
- **Criterion added: the mean width must be below one standard deviation of the
  target.** Not met today (4 to 5); phase 6.3 (a surrogate with a varying `σ`) is the
  way to meet it. Until then, no document may present the bound as informative.
- **Coverage under selection**: not guaranteed and measured at 0.879; a valid procedure
  is phase 6.4.
