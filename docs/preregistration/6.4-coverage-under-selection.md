# 6.4 — Conformal coverage under selection

- **Frozen on**: not yet (draft).
- **Hypothesis**: a selection-valid procedure (conformal selection, Jin & Candès 2023, or
  weighted conformal with randomised selection, Fannjiang et al. 2022) restores coverage
  ≥ 1 − α on the plans Frank-Wolfe selects, where the passive calibration covers 76.0 %
  [71.9 ; 80.0] at a nominal 90 %.
- **Primary metric**: empirical coverage on selected plans, re-evaluated by the reference
  oracle, with its Clopper-Pearson interval; accepted with
  `CoverageReport.within(ACCEPTANCE_BAND)` (0.86 – 0.94 at α = 0.10).
- **Secondary**: mean width in units of the target standard deviation (criterion < 1).
- **Test**: coverage inside the band in both regimes (exchangeable and selected); paired
  Wilcoxon on 20 calibration draws for the difference.
- **Sample size**: 400 plans × 4 azimuths × 10 repetitions, as in `j5_selection.py`.
- **Success criterion**: both regimes inside the band, width < 1 target standard deviation.
- **Failure means**: the certificate keeps `regime = "selected"` without a coverage figure.
