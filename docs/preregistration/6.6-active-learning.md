# 6.6 — Active against random acquisition

- **Frozen on**: not yet (draft).
- **Hypothesis**: uncertainty × density acquisition yields a narrower final conformal
  interval than random acquisition at equal budget (B5: 16/30 campaigns favourable,
  paired Wilcoxon p = 0.53 on a 48-plan pool).
- **Primary metric**: final mean conformal width, paired per campaign.
- **Test**: paired Wilcoxon, two-sided, α = 0.05; equivalence with
  `bench.stats.tost` if not significant.
- **Sample size**: at least 30 campaigns; pool and budget fixed with `bench.stats.power`
  for a 5 % width reduction at power 0.8.
- **Seeds**: corrected derived seeds, `derive(17, "6.6/<campaign>")`.
- **Success criterion**: significant reduction, or equivalence shown by TOST; either way
  the result is published.
