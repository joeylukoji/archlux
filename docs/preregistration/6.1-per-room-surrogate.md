# 6.1 — Per-room surrogate

- **Frozen on**: not yet (draft).
- **Hypothesis**: a surrogate that returns one value per room, optimised through an
  explicit scalarisation, removes the degenerate rooms that the plan-level surrogate
  produces (B9: 20 of 48 variants push a room to the 0.50 m floor).
- **Primary metric**: share of Frank-Wolfe variants with a room whose smaller side equals
  the regulatory `min_width`, per scalarisation (area-weighted mean, minimum over rooms,
  share of rooms above a threshold).
- **Test**: exact McNemar, per-room surrogate against plan-level surrogate, same plans and
  azimuths, two-sided, α = 0.05, Holm across the three scalarisations.
- **Sample size**: the 6 B9 plans × 8 azimuths are too few; at least 40 generated plans
  × 8 azimuths, the number fixed with `bench.stats.power` before the run.
- **Seeds / split**: `derive(17, "6.1/<plan>/<azimuth>")`; surrogate trained on sites
  disjoint from the optimised plans.
- **Success criterion**: no degenerate room in `results/orientation/` with the regulatory
  `min_width`, and fewer saturated variants than the plan-level surrogate (Holm-adjusted
  p < 0.05).
- **Failure means**: J9 stays suspended and the paper keeps the plan-level surrogate as a
  stated limitation.
